# Super Mario Kart ZX Attribution

- Original Super Mario Kart ZX fan game: srPerez
- HTML5/WebAssembly port: burnedpopcorn
- Upstream web-port repository: https://github.com/burnedpopcorn/SM-Kart-ZX-Web-Port
- Original game page: https://srperez.itch.io/smk-zx

ARCADIA incorporates the compiled web port with permission the ARCADIA project owner confirmed directly with its author. The original game files, presentation, characters, music, and gameplay remain credited to their respective creators and rights holders.

ARCADIA adds the surrounding arcade launch flow, same-origin input and audio bridge, mobile touch controls, responsive presentation, dashboard card, and local player progression. The bundled GameMaker data also includes narrow fixes for the upstream web port's broken course-quit restart, fragile post-race transitions, and hidden Grand Prix reset selection.

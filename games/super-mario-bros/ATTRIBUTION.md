# Super Mario Bros HTML5 campaign

ARCADIA's embedded campaign is based on the PlayMario HTML5 Client:

- Source: https://github.com/PlayMario/HTML5_Client
- Playable upstream site: https://playmario.github.io/HTML5_Client/
- Upstream branch used: `gh-pages`

The ARCADIA integration keeps the original 32 numbered campaign maps and removes access to the random map generator, mod controls, map picker, and upstream page chrome. The ARCADIA owner has stated that the upstream maintainer personally granted permission for this use.
